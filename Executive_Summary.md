# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: CLINICAL DATA WAREHOUSE & PATHWAY ANALYTICS

**Prepared By:** Shubham Parikh
**Project Focus:** Healthcare Operations, Readmission Tracking, and Resource Utilization

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
The objective of this initiative was to engineer a clinical data warehouse capable of transforming raw Electronic Health Record (EHR) data into strategic intelligence. By migrating disparate hospital records into an optimized dimensional model, this pipeline enables hospital administrators to track patient care pathways, monitor financial risk tied to 30-day readmissions, and optimize clinical resource allocation.

## 2. ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY
* **Data Modeling:** Deployed a Star Schema featuring a central fact table supported by dimensions for patients, dates, diagnoses, and admission types, establishing a single source of truth for hospital operations.
* **Journey Mapping:** Applied recursive analytical modeling to track multi-hop patient care journeys, sequentially mapping the departments a patient traverses from initial admission to final discharge.
* **Automated KPI Engine:** Engineered a suite of executable procedures to instantly aggregate operational metrics, replacing manual ad-hoc reporting with an automated, dashboard-ready data feed.

## 3. ANALYTICAL CAPABILITIES & EXTRACTED INSIGHTS
The pipeline operationalizes the following strategic capabilities:
* **30-Day Readmission Tracking:** Automatically flags patients readmitted within a 30-day window. Cross-referencing these cohorts against insurance providers and demographic data exposes high-risk, high-cost segments.
* **Length of Stay (LOS) Optimization:** Isolates overall and ICU-specific LOS, breaking down facility utilization by age groups and disease categories to reveal drivers of prolonged hospitalization.
* **Care Quality Metrics:** Tracks in-hospital mortality rates linked to specific admission locations to evaluate care efficacy under varying hospital load conditions.
* **Care Pathway Bottlenecks:** Identifies the most frequent admission-to-discharge sequences, highlighting operational bottlenecks in internal patient flow.

## 4. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
Based on the pipeline's architectural outputs, the following operational shifts are recommended:
1. **Targeted Intervention Deployment:** Utilize the readmission tracking logic to trigger post-discharge follow-up protocols specifically for identified high-risk cohorts, actively reducing preventable readmissions.
2. **Dynamic Resource Allocation:** Align staffing schedules and ICU bed availability with the monthly admission trends and demographic LOS profiles generated by the KPI engine.
3. **Executive Dashboard Integration:** Connect the established data endpoints directly to visualization platforms to provide department heads with live, interactive access to facility utilization metrics.
